Release 2026-10-11.1 · 2026-10-11

CHIRPS v3.0 adopted: every cycle recalibrated and re-verified (2026 forecasts retrospective)

Change to the scientific method New verification observations Rainfall-domain presentation update New evidence or diagnostics (method unchanged)

  • Change to the scientific method: Observations changed from CHIRPS v2.0 (daily p25) to CHIRPS v3.0 (official monthly 0.05 degree product averaged onto the 0.25 degree grid; Funk et al. 2026). The whole chain was refitted with the same method: amount correction, terciles, blend weights, historical evaluation, development runs and decision gates (which give the same outcome as with v2.0).
  • New verification observations: The 2026 forecasts of all four cycles were recalibrated with CHIRPS v3.0 after their outcomes were known and are verified against CHIRPS v3.0 (FMAM Feb-May and season, JJAS Jun-Aug). They are retrospective reconstructions; the CHIRPS v2.0 versions are release 2026-10-10.2.
  • Rainfall-domain presentation update: The FMAM rainfall contribution domain keeps its rule (FMAM >= 100 mm and >= 20% of annual) on the CHIRPS v3.0 climatology: 75.6% of Ethiopia (72.6% with v2.0). The JJAS R1+R2 and ONDJ R3 regime domains are unchanged; the regimes come from the CHIRPS v2.0 daily climatology.
  • New evidence or diagnostics (method unchanged): The v2.0 vs v3.0 comparison (climatology about 15% wetter with v3.0, seasonal shares and year rankings closely agreeing; calibration skill similar overall) is in Methods and docs/45.

Cycles: FMAM 2026 (January initialization), FMAM 2026 (February initialization), JJAS 2026, ONDJ 2026/27

Differences from the current release

  • Forecast numbers, statuses and verification are unchanged; differences are in presentation only.

Release 2026-10-10.1 · 2026-10-10

FMAM 2026 verified; February initialization; FMAM rainfall contribution domain; outlooks scored against CHIRPS

New verification observations Rainfall-domain presentation update New evidence or diagnostics (method unchanged) Display or explanatory-text change

  • New verification observations: February 2026 and the full FMAM 2026 season verified for both initializations. CHIRPS never published the February by_month file; February is taken from the official annual p25 file after checking it is identical to the published by_month files of January and March 2026.
  • Rainfall-domain presentation update: Main FMAM area: the FMAM rainfall contribution domain (FMAM >= 100 mm and >= 20% of annual rainfall, CHIRPS 1993-2025), replacing the earlier R2-based domain; the FMAM main-season domain (>= 40% of annual) is a second view. The earlier R2-based verification report is archived as superseded.
  • New evidence or diagnostics (method unchanged): The platform, EMI and ICPAC are scored against CHIRPS for each one's own period (single season): favoured-category matches with observation, outlook and favoured-category coverage, and RPSS for the platform and EMI. Observed CHIRPS terciles are shown alongside the three outlooks. Comparison results are computed separately for every domain view.
  • Display or explanatory-text change: Verification reports state the cycle's own initialization and season; one current report per cycle. Comparison maps draw the national border once. Historical performance opens with a takeaway; technical detail is expandable.
